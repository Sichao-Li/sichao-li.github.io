Two research themes.

Selected work is grouped by the contribution it makes, rather than by chronology.

  1. R01

    AI Governance

    I develop technical foundations for governing AI through inspectable evidence: methods that make model behaviour interpretable and explainable, test whether reasoning is faithful, monitor what systems understand and rely on, and evaluate whether agents act for defensible reasons.
